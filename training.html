Tata Play Fiber – Customer Service Training

1️⃣ What is Tata Play Fiber?
Tata Play Fiber is a high-speed broadband internet service provided by Tata Group in India. It uses fiber-optic cables (FTTH) to give fast, stable internet for homes and offices.
2️⃣ What does FTTH mean?
FTTH means Fiber To The Home.
  • Higher speed
  • Better stability
  • Less internet drop
3️⃣ What is Internet?
Internet is a network that allows devices to:
  • Browse websites
  • Watch videos
  • Use apps like YouTube, WhatsApp, Netflix
4️⃣ What equipment is installed at customer’s home?
  • Fiber cable
  • ONT / Modem (converts fiber signal to internet)
  • Wi-Fi Router (shares internet wirelessly)
5️⃣ Difference between Wi-Fi and Internet?
Internet: Service from Tata Play Fiber
Wi-Fi: Wireless signal from router
Internet can work even if Wi-Fi has issues.
6️⃣ Common customer complaints?
  • Internet not working
  • Slow internet speed
  • Wi-Fi connected but no internet
  • Red / LOS light blinking
  • Plan expired
  • Payment done but service inactive
  • Relocation request
7️⃣ What does “Internet not working” usually mean?
  • Fiber cut
  • Power issue
  • Modem/router switched off
  • Area outage
  • Plan expired
8️⃣ What is slow internet?
Internet works but speed is low due to:
  • Too many connected devices
  • Wi-Fi distance
  • Background downloads
  • Router issue
9️⃣ What is LOS / Red light?
LOS (Loss of Signal) red light means fiber signal problem. Usually requires technician visit.
🔟 What is ETA?
ETA = Estimated Time of Arrival / Resolution
Time given to customer for issue resolution.
1️⃣1️⃣ What is TAT?
TAT = Turn Around Time
Maximum allowed time to close a complaint.
1️⃣2️⃣ What is a complaint / ticket?
A ticket is an official record of the customer’s problem created for tracking and resolution.
1️⃣3️⃣ What is escalation?
When an issue is not solved within TAT, it is moved to a higher support level.
1️⃣4️⃣ What is relocation?
Shifting the internet connection from one address to another.
1️⃣5️⃣ What is plan expiry?
When customer validity ends and internet stops until recharge.
1️⃣6️⃣ Outbound vs Inbound vs Chat?
  • Inbound: Customer calls you
  • Outbound: You call customer
  • Chat: Customer messages, you reply by typing
1️⃣7️⃣ Customer service’s main role?
  • Understand customer problem
  • Follow process
  • Provide correct solution
  • Keep customer calm and satisfied
