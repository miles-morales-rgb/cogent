What is Customer Service?

Customer service means helping customers by understanding their issue, following process, and providing correct solutions politely.

Main Responsibilities

  • Handle calls or chats
  • Identify customer problem
  • Create tickets if required
  • Follow ETA & TAT

Support Channels

  • Inbound Calls
  • Outbound Calls
  • Chat Support
