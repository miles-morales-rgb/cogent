4️⃣ Common Customer Issues & Replies

🔴 Internet Not Working
Please check if the router power light is ON and all cables are connected. Let me know the status so I can assist further.
🐢 Slow Internet
Please restart the router and test speed with one device. If issue persists, I will check from my end.
💰 Plan Expiry
Your plan expires on [date]. You can recharge via Tata Play app or website.
🏠 Relocation
Relocation is subject to feasibility and applicable charges. Shall I raise a request for you?
